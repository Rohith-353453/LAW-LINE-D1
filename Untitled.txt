## 3. Problem Statement

India has a large and complex legal system, but:

* Citizens lack awareness of their legal rights
* Laws are difficult to understand (language + structure)
* Legal consultation is expensive and intimidating
* Vulnerable groups face exploitation due to misinformation
* Access to reliable legal information is limited

As a result, **law exists on paper but is inaccessible in practice**.

---

## 4. Project Objective

To build a **web-based, AI-assisted legal awareness platform** that:

* Helps citizens understand whether their problem is legal
* Explains rights and procedures in simple language
* Uses **only verified government legal sources**
* Protects user privacy
* Does **not** provide legal advice or case predictions

---

## 5. Scope of the Project

### In Scope

* Legal awareness and education
* Problem-based legal explainers
* User-group-based guidance
* AI-assisted search and classification
* Step-by-step procedural clarity

### Out of Scope

* Legal advice
* Case outcome prediction
* Lawyer recommendation
* Court filing automation
* Storage of personal data

---

## 6. Reference Model

The project structure is inspired by **Nyaaya**, particularly:

* Legal Explainers
* User-based categorization
* State-wise legal resources

However, this project **extends Nyaaya’s model** with:

* AI-powered search and understanding
* Rule-engine-controlled AI
* Privacy-first backend architecture

---

## 7. Core Website Sections

### 7.1 Legal Explainers

Problem-based legal information grouped into categories such as:

* Family & Marriage
* Money & Property
* Crimes & Violence
* Police & Courts
* Labour & Employment
* Health & Environment
* Citizen Rights & Constitution
* Government & Elections
* Media & Intellectual Property

Each explainer contains:

* Issue description (plain language)
* Applicable law names (no sections unless needed)
* Rights and duties
* Step-by-step procedures
* Official sources

---

### 7.2 Users (Context-Aware Guidance)

Legal guidance adapted for user groups such as:

* General
* Children
* Women
* LGBTQIA+
* Employees
* Persons with Disabilities
* Scheduled Castes & Tribes
* Senior Citizens
* Informal Workers

⚠️ No personal identity is stored.
User group selection is **voluntary and session-based only**.

---

### 7.3 State-Wise Resources

* State-specific rules and authorities
* District-level references (future scope)
* Localized legal procedures

---

## 8. Legal Knowledge Sources

All legal content is derived **only from official and authoritative sources**:

* **India Code** – Central & State Acts
* **Gazette of India** – Amendments and notifications
* **Ministry of Law & Justice** publications

The platform **summarizes and explains** laws; it does not republish full legal texts.

## 9. System Architecture (High Level)

```
User
 ↓
Frontend (Web UI)
 ↓
Backend API (FastAPI – Python)
 ↓
AI Layer (Gemini – understanding & simplification)
 ↓
Rule Engine (Policy & safety control)
 ↓
Verified Legal Knowledge Base
 ↓
Formatted Legal Guidance
```

---

## 10. Role of Artificial Intelligence

### AI Is Used For:

* Understanding user queries in natural language
* Classifying issues into legal categories
* Improving search relevance
* Simplifying legal explanations

### AI Is NOT Used For:

* Giving legal advice
* Interpreting law independently
* Predicting legal outcomes
* Making binding decisions

> AI functions as a **language and understanding assistant**, not a legal authority.

---

## 11. Rule Engine (Accountability Layer)

### Definition

A **rule engine** is a control layer that governs how AI outputs are used.

### Responsibilities

* Validate AI classification results
* Allow only predefined legal categories
* Enforce confidence thresholds
* Prevent advisory or prescriptive language
* Attach mandatory disclaimers
* Ensure mapping to verified legal data

### Implementation Language

* **Python**, inside FastAPI backend

---

## 13. Privacy & Security Design

### Data We Do NOT Collect

* Names
* Phone numbers
* Aadhaar
* Location
* IP-based profiling
* Case details

### Privacy Measures

* Anonymous query handling
* Input anonymization before AI calls
* No cookies or tracking
* No long-term storage of queries

### Design Philosophy

* Privacy-by-design
* Ethical AI usage
* Alignment with India’s DPDP principles

---

## 14. User Flow (End-to-End)

1. User selects a legal category or user group
2. User enters a problem in plain language
3. AI analyzes and classifies the query
4. Rule engine validates AI output
5. Verified legal content is retrieved
6. AI simplifies explanation
7. User receives structured legal awareness

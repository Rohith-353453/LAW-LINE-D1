Police & Courts

Simple explanations of police procedures, arrests, bail, and court processes.

What Happens After Arrest?

Rights of arrested person.

  • Must be informed of grounds of arrest.
  • Right to inform family/friend.
  • Produced before magistrate within 24 hours.
  • Right to legal aid.

Reference: Article 22 Constitution & CrPC

How to Apply for Bail?

Bail in bailable and non-bailable offences.

  • Bailable: Right to bail.
  • Non-bailable: Apply to court with grounds.
  • Anticipatory bail available in some cases.

Reference: Sections 436-439 CrPC

Health & Environment

Simple explanations of laws on medical rights, mental health, pollution, and environmental protection.

Medical Negligence

Patients have right to proper treatment.

  • File complaint with State Medical Council.
  • Approach consumer court for compensation.
  • Free treatment in government hospitals.

Reference: Indian Medical Council Act & Consumer Protection Act

Mental Health Rights

Protected under Mental Healthcare Act.

  • Right to treatment with dignity.
  • Advance directive for treatment preferences.
  • Nominate representative.

Reference: Mental Healthcare Act, 2017

Pollution Complaint

Right to clean environment.

  • File complaint with Pollution Control Board.
  • Approach NGT for serious issues.
  • Public can file PIL.

Reference: Environment Protection Act, 1986
